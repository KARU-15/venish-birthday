Tamari sathe life na badha phases jovaa chhe — hasvu chhe, ladvu chhe, manavu chhe, ek bijane irritate karva chhe, ane pachhi fari ek bijane hug kari ne badhu bhuli javu chhe. 🫶🏻

Because I don't just want to be a part of your happy moments… I want to be there for your whole life. ❤️

3800 km aapda vachche chhe, pan mara dil ma tame ekdum najik cho.
